Describe a song.
Get its name.

Instruments, mood, tempo, era, where you heard it — anything but the name.

Throw in whatever you remember: a line of the lyrics, the name, instruments, who's singing, tempo, genre, the era, where it's from. Quotes insist on the words. Exact syntax, if you want it.

Add your music
to the index

Nothing is uploaded. Your browser reads the files right here on your computer, identifies each one by fingerprint, and boils it down to a 2 KB list of numbers describing how it sounds. Only those numbers and the artist / title / album text are sent. The site has no way to receive audio at all.

  • Leave the tab open. Other tabs are fine; closing this one stops the scan.
  • Pick up where you left off. Finished tracks are kept and skipped next time — in any browser.
  • Wrong folder? Non-audio files are ignored, and there's a Stop button.
  • Obscure stuff welcome. Tracks the fingerprint database doesn't know are kept with your tags.
  • Two passes, and the second is optional. First every track gets indexed by sound, which is when searching starts working. Then, for songs no lyrics site has words for — usually a quarter or so of a library — your browser listens and works out the words itself, so a line you remember can find them. That part downloads a second model and takes about ten seconds a track. Stop whenever; everything finished is kept.
